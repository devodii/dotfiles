## Chunk 1: The Visual & Narrative Architecture

The Layout (Mobile-First)
Whitespace Rule: One sentence per line. Never use paragraphs.
The "See More" Trap: The first 3 lines must be so punchy that the reader is forced to click "See More."
Scannability: Use 3-5 bullet points for the core details. The human brain scans for these.
The Skeleton Format

```md
**[Bold Hook: Under 8 words. Attacks a myth or states a result]**

[Short context line 1]
[Short context line 2]

- [Concrete detail 1]
- [Concrete detail 2]
- [Concrete detail 3]

[The Pivot: A sharp bridge from the story to the business reality]

[The Current State: 2 lines on what you are doing/solving now]

[The CTA: A short, low-friction invitation to act or follow]
```

## Chunk 2: The Plain English Protocol & Tone Guardrails

The "No-Fluff" Language Rule

- Zero Jargon: Never use words like "primitives," "ecosystems," "leverage," or "synergy."
- Simple Sentences: Use direct, declarative English. If a 10-year-old can't understand the sentence, it is too complex.
- Punctuation: No em-dashes (—). Use periods. Short sentences are more powerful than long ones connected by dashes.

The "Anti-Robotic" Tone

- No Cliches: Never use metaphors like "The race isn't over," "Building the future," or "Scaling the ladder."
- No Corporate Throat-clearing: Never start with "I hope this finds you well" or "I wanted to share my thoughts on..."
- Fact-Based Narrative: Use raw facts (numbers, dates, specific items) to build emotion rather than using adjectives (hard, difficult, amazing).

The Pivot Constraint

- The 2-Line Limit: You have exactly two lines to bridge your personal story to your business goal.
- The "So What" Test: The pivot must answer why the reader should care about your story in the context of their own problems.
- Founder-to-Founder: Speak like a person who ships code and makes decisions, not a person who writes marketing copy.

The "Algorithm" Best Practices

- The "Native" Link: Never use preview cards for links. Use bare text URLs at the very bottom.
- Comment Baiting: If the goal is engagement, move the main link to the first comment and tell the reader it is there.
- Raw Media: Use unedited, phone-quality photos or raw screenshots. Polished stock photos kill reach.

Final Skill Summary

- Hook: Bold, under 8 words.
- Structure: One sentence per line.
- Tone: Plain English, no jargon, no em-dashes.
- Pivot: Two-line bridge from past to present.
- CTA: Low-friction invitation to follow or DM.
